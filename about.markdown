Hi, I'm Ellie! I'm 11 years old and live in Texas — right under the Central Flyway, where countless birds pass through every year.

I was born in the U.S., moved to China when I was 4, and came back in 2024 when I was 10. That's when I started birding with my mom, and now I can't stop noticing all the colors, songs, and surprises in nature.

I love watching birds, taking photos, writing nature notes, and drawing. This website is where I keep track of everything I see and learn — one bird and one adventure at a time.
