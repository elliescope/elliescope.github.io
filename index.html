Hi, I'm Ellie! 🦜

I'm 11 years old and live in Texas — right under the amazing Central Flyway where so many amazing birds pass by every year!

I was born in the U.S., moved to China when I was 4, and came back in 2024 when I was 10. That's when I started birding with my mom — and now I can't stop noticing all the colors, songs, and surprises in nature.

Here you'll find my birding checklist, photos, nature notes, and art — all the things I love to create and share.

This is my little corner of the world — where I keep learning about nature, one bird and one adventure at a time.🌿
